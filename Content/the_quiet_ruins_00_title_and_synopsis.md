# THE QUIET RUINS
### A Hogwarts fan fiction

## Back-cover synopsis

Decades after a war between Muggles and the wizarding world shattered Hogwarts, Mara Hallowell arrives from Connecticut with a map, a list of Severus Snape’s former haunts, and a conviction that magic belongs to whoever is brave enough to take it.

The railway ends before the castle. Beyond it lie drowned paths, watching ghosts, feral house-elves, and creatures that have made the abandoned grounds their own. Somewhere inside the ruins are Snape’s notes and the magical objects he left behind. Mara intends to learn their secrets—and to become more than the powerless outsider she has always felt herself to be.

But Hogwarts has been waiting a long time. And the things that survived the war may not want to be found.
